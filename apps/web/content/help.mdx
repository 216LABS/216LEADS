## What the score means

Every home gets a score from 0 to 100 for your kind of work. Higher means more of the things that make a home need you are true: its age, recent sales, permits that are due, code violations and more. Homes at your threshold or above, or where something just happened (a sale, a nearby job), show up as leads.

## How reasons work

Each lead lists the public records behind its score, with how many points each one added and a link to the record. Nothing is guessed: if we don't have the data for a home, the reason isn't shown.

## Marking homes contacted, won or skipped

Use the buttons on a lead to mark it contacted, quoted, won, lost or skipped. Skipping removes a home from your list. Won and lost homes teach the scoring what works for you (see Settings, How we score) and feed your Results page.

## Postcards and credits

Mail one home, a selection, or a whole block from the map. Plans include monthly postcard credits; after that each postcard is $0.99 on your card. You can cancel a send for 15 minutes. Homes that opted out, or were mailed in the last 30 days, are skipped automatically.

## Auto-mail

In Mail, turn on auto-mail to send a postcard to every new home above a score you pick, with a weekly count and a monthly dollar cap. An optional follow-up goes out on day 21.

## Changing plan or canceling

Go to [Settings, Billing](/settings/billing) to change plan, update your card, see invoices or cancel.

## Homeowner opt-outs

Any homeowner can stop mail at [216labs.dev/homesignal/optout](/optout). That address is then skipped for every business, and the link is printed on every postcard.

## Where the data comes from

See [the public records we use](/data), and Settings, Data coverage for the towns in your area.
